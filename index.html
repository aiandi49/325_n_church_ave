Development Vision

Three Paths to
Extraordinary
Returns

Our team has identified three highest-and-best-use development scenarios, each aligned with Tucson's dual C-3/O-3 zoning and supported by current market fundamentals. Hover to explore each concept.

01

Concept 01 · Strongest Investment Segment

Medical-Centric
Mixed-Use

A multi-story building featuring ground-floor “med-tail” — urgent care, physical therapy — with professional medical suites above. Medical office is currently the strongest investment segment in Tucson due to population growth and an aging demographic. Leverages O-3 for medical service, C-3 for retail/food support.

C-3 + O-3 Zoning Compliant
02

Concept 02 · High-Demand Residential

High-Density
Multifamily &
Ground-Floor Retail

A mid-rise luxury apartment complex (4–6 stories) featuring a Southwestern Tucson aesthetic with ground-floor coffee shop or fitness studio. Residents increasingly seek turnkey, move-in-ready homes in connected communities. A 2-acre parcel supports significant units with required parking.

Permitted in C-3 & O-3
03

Concept 03 · Expanding Sector

Adaptive “Flex”
Professional Hub

A modern office/creative campus designed for engineering and government contractors who require secure, collaborative spaces — sectors currently expanding in Tucson. Tenants pay premiums for modern infrastructure including better HVAC and flexible floor plates.

C-3 Technical Service · R&D
